# Business Insights and Recommendations

## Overview

The SQL analysis was performed across EV registrations,
EV sales, charging stations, and charging sessions to
identify important business patterns and potential
infrastructure gaps.

---

# Key Insights

## 1. EV Adoption vs Charging Infrastructure

### Finding

The analysis identified states with above-average EV registrations but below-average charging-station availability, indicating a potential gap between EV adoption and charging infrastructure.

Example:

"State X recorded high EV registrations while having
relatively fewer charging stations compared with the
overall state average."

### Business Meaning

This may indicate that charging infrastructure has not
grown at the same pace as EV adoption.

---

## 2. EV Sales vs Charging Infrastructure

### Finding

Several states recorded above-average EV sales while having below-average charging-station availability, indicating strong EV market activity relative to existing infrastructure.

### Business Meaning

States with strong EV sales and relatively low charging
infrastructure may require closer monitoring of
charging capacity.

---

## 3. EV Registrations per Charging Station

### Finding

The analysis identified states with the highest number of EV registrations per charging station, indicating relatively greater EV demand per available charging station.

### Business Meaning

A high value indicates that a larger number of EV
registrations are associated with each charging station.

---

## 4. EV Sales per Charging Station

### Finding

The analysis identified states with the highest EV sales per charging station, showing strong vehicle sales relative to available charging infrastructure.

### Business Meaning

A high value indicates strong EV sales relative to
available charging infrastructure.

---

## 5. Charging Station Utilization

### Finding

The analysis identified charging stations with high charging-session activity relative to their available chargers, indicating comparatively higher utilization.
### Business Meaning

Stations with high session activity relative to
available chargers may experience greater demand.

---

## 6. EV Sales and Charging Revenue

### Finding

The analysis identified states performing above the overall average in both EV sales and charging revenue, indicating strong activity across both EV sales and charging services.

### Business Meaning

These states demonstrate strong activity across both
EV sales and charging revenue.

---

## 7. Potential Infrastructure Need

### Finding

The final analysis identified states with above-average EV registrations, EV sales, and charging-session activity while having below-average charging-station availability. These states represent potential areas where charging infrastructure may require further evaluation.

### Business Meaning

States showing high EV registrations, high EV sales,
high charging activity, and relatively low station
availability may represent potential areas for
infrastructure expansion.

---

# Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

1. Evaluate charging infrastructure expansion in states
   showing strong EV adoption and sales.

2. Monitor highly utilized charging stations for
   potential capacity constraints.

3. Compare charging infrastructure growth with EV
   adoption and sales trends.

4. Prioritize further analysis of states where multiple
   demand indicators are simultaneously high.

5. Continue monitoring charging revenue and session
   activity to understand infrastructure performance.
