# Business Problem

## Project Overview

The EV Charging Analysis project analyzes electric vehicle registrations,
EV sales, charging station infrastructure, and charging session activity
using SQL Server.

The objective is to understand the relationship between EV adoption,
EV sales, charging infrastructure, and charging demand.

---

## Business Objective

The main objective is to identify patterns that can help businesses
understand:

- EV adoption across states
- EV sales performance
- Charging infrastructure availability
- Charging station utilization
- Charging demand and revenue
- Potential infrastructure gaps

---

## Business Questions

The analysis focuses on questions such as:

1. Which states have high EV registrations but relatively low
   charging infrastructure?

2. Which states have high EV sales but comparatively low
   charging infrastructure?

3. Which states have the highest EV registrations per charging station?

4. Which states have the highest EV sales per charging station?

5. Which charging stations have the highest utilization?

6. Which states contribute significantly to both EV sales
   and charging revenue?

7. Which states have high EV adoption, high EV sales,
   but relatively low charging infrastructure?

8. Which states show a potential need for additional
   charging infrastructure?

---

## Data Sources

The project uses four  datasets created for
SQL analysis and practice:

- EV_REGISTRATIONS
- CHARGING_STATIONS
- EV_SALES
- CHARGING_SESSIONS
